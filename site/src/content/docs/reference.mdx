<Callout kind="note" title="Version 0.9.0">

This reference describes Reactive CSS 0.9.0. Reactive CSS is not standard CSS: its runtime reads these extensions from your stylesheets and interprets them. 0.9 adds event rules with `:on()`, state mutations, `event()`, drag gestures, `bind()` and `persist()` in `state()`, `--attr-*`, `content` as DOM text and `:blur`. The reference lists the 0.9 syntax only. The 0.8 declarations keep working; their pages are listed under [Legacy 0.8.x compatibility](/docs/migration/#legacy-08x-compatibility).

</Callout>

<Callout kind="note" title="Status labels">

Features marked `exp` are experimental: `persist()`, `content` as DOM text, drag gestures and the drag fields of `event()`, the `--attr-*` namespace, `typed()`, `element()`, the `attr()`, `pick()` and `split()` expressions, `focus:`, `prevent()` and `state(…, bind(<attribute>))`. `wave()` was removed in 0.5.0.

`:on()` and its mutations, `bind(value)` and `bind(checked)` in `state()`, `:blur`, and the pointer, keyboard and form fields of `event()` are shown as stable, following the 0.9.0 release recommendation, although the runtime's `describe()` still reports them as experimental. Everything else is stable.

</Callout>

## Beyond CSS
- **JavaScript API:** `start()`, `stop()`, `refresh()`, `stats()`, `diagnostics`, `describe()`, `compile()` and `version`, and the `ReactiveCSSConfig` options: see [Installation](/docs/installation/#es-module).
- **Custom bundles and the `reactive-css` CLI** (0.8.0): features, profiles, `build`, `analyze` (which also lists 0.8 syntax with its 0.9 form) and missing-feature diagnostics: see [Custom bundles](/docs/custom-bundles/).

## Not shipped
These are recorded in the library's feature registry as planned, deferred, rejected or removed. None of them is available in 0.9.0. The list comes from `describe()`.
