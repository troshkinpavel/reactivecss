Tabs

Tabs selected by click or arrow keys, with the ARIA tab pattern written from one state.

One state on the tab set, --active, picks the tab and the panel.
:nth-var(--active) matches the tab and the panel at that position.
Arrow keys move the selection and the focus, as the ARIA tab pattern expects.
