## How it works
1. `.menu` declares `--open` plus two dismiss triggers: `outside(click, …)` and `key(Escape, …)`.
2. The trigger button toggles the state.
3. `ul:when(--open)` shows the list.

## Consider popover first
The native `popover` attribute gives light dismiss, Escape handling and top-layer stacking with no script.

## Accessibility
<Callout kind="warning" title="Visual only">

A complete menu also needs `aria-expanded`, focus moved into the list, arrow keys and focus returned on close.

</Callout>
