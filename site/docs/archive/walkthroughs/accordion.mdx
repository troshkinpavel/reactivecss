## How it works
1. Every `.item` owns its own `--open`.
2. The item's button toggles it.
3. `.body:when(--open)` shows the panel and `when()` rotates the chevron.

## Consider native HTML first
`<details>` and `<summary>` need no script and are accessible by default. Use state when you need behavior they can't give you, such as styling the whole item from the open state.
